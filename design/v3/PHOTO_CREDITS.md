## Required release attribution

Even when not mandatory, credit every original photographer in the app About/Credits page and repository. For the Honda photograph, provide copyright-holder attribution, image source link, link to CC BY-SA 4.0, indicate changes (resize/colour adjustment) and ensure modifications to the image follow the share-alike condition.

The source photo for the Ford is US-spec; year, generation, colour and trim are not defined in the demo. The demo also includes **two motorcycles**, which must appear in Fleet.

## Production limitations

- Source-page rights and credit information has been reviewed; an actual downloadable image is only ready after a successful image fetch, dimension check, and visual review.
- The browser prototype may use local photo derivatives once generated. Do not hotlink Wikimedia in production Qt; use the Qt resource system after approval.
- Commercial intent does not determine copyright permission. Model names are used descriptively; no carmaker endorsement.
- Images containing visible license plates, people, locations or dealership marks may need cropping or exclusion after inspection.
- Image modifications and compression should remove EXIF location metadata.
