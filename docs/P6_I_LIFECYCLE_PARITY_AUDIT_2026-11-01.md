This review is source analysis, not an extra test run or physical owner signoff.

### Status definitions

- **E (scoped evidenced):** C++/QML feature and meaningful automated native regression exist in fictional-only configuration. Does **not** imply commercial/physical approval.
- **P (partial or UX difference):** meaningful operator equivalence, context, screen composition or edge-case remains missing/unproven.
- **D (deliberate divergence):** redesigned UX or privacy restriction; requires preservation/owner decision, **not necessarily a code defect**.
- **R (release design):** intentionally outside P6 fictional demo; solve under P7–P9, **not** by silently enabling real data.

**Audit finding:** **Three material presentational/operator areas need explicit resolution:** (A) recent activity absent on Qt Quick Overview despite the selected product design; (B) legacy Dashboard's utilisation/type counts are not individually surfaced in current native Overview; (C) legacy Rentals table shows more date/contract context and customer contact than the privacy-safe Qt Quick list. Their treatment differs: A has a source-backed design rationale for a bounded read-only feature; B should be accepted or deliberately excluded from the modern hierarchy; C needs a purpose-limited contract-detail design and explicit privacy authorization, not an unreviewed PII role.

No source-backed evidence of a missing **core Fleet create/search/book/return/archive/delete** transaction path was found in this bounded audit, but that is **not proof of exhaustive full lifecycle parity** or of all negative-case/manual acceptance. The provisional ninth P6 group remains open.
